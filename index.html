Why Parker Beckett exists

A music project built to last.

The project began as a creative outlet. It became a professional home for songs Corey realized were worth preserving.

After years in radio, he trusted his instincts about what made a song work. The lyrics came from his own life, but releasing them under his own name did not feel right, and hearing someone else sing such personal material felt even stranger. Parker Beckett became the place where those stories could live.

The project is first for Parker and Beckett, and someday their children. It is also for anyone who recognizes part of their own life in the material.
