Story Behind the Song

The Story Behind “After All”

From a Poem to a Song

I was lying in bed one night, couldn't sleep, and decided to check Facebook Messenger.

There was a message from my friend Jeremy. He'd sent me a screenshot of a poem he was working on.

At the time, I'd been thinking about music for In His Footsteps, a screenplay I'd written. I knew I was going to need some kind of music for the movie, and I was starting to wonder whether I could put an album together for it.

So when Jeremy's poem showed up, I started thinking.

There's a character in In His Footsteps named Justin, and I wondered whether I could take some of what Jeremy had written and work it into Justin's story.

The poem wasn't really long enough to make a complete song on its own. But I thought maybe I could take those words, bring in parts of the screenplay, and see what happened.

So I gave it a shot.

And it wasn't bad.

It wasn't anywhere close to finished, either. But there was something there, and I liked the idea of taking two different pieces of writing and finding a way to make them work together.

That got me interested in trying other things.

I started working on more songs, pulling from different situations and ideas, and thinking about what an album might sound like. What started as an experiment with Jeremy's poem had gotten me writing music in a way I hadn't really planned.

Eventually, I came back to "After All."

I spent more time working on it, figuring out what belonged in the song and how to bring everything together. And little by little, it became what it is now.

There's still a pretty strong mental health theme running through it. And even though the song came from a combination of Jeremy's writing and a fictional character in my screenplay, it still hits pretty hard when I listen to it.

I think if somebody knew Justin's story from In His Footsteps, they'd recognize some of the connections.

Maybe we'll get to use "After All" in a movie someday. I'd like that.

But for now, it's a song that started because I couldn't sleep, checked my messages, and found a poem my friend was working on.

I had no idea where it would go.

I'm glad I decided to find out.
