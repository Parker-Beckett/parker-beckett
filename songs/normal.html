Story Behind the Song

The Story Behind “Normal”

Who Decides What's Normal?

What the hell is normal, anyway?

That's a question I started asking myself after being diagnosed with symptoms of ADHD.

Around that same time, someone had also talked to me about something called "high-IQ ADHD." I started learning more about the way my brain works, and suddenly, a lot of things about myself started making sense.

It was one of those moments where you look back at your life and think, well, that explains a few things.

But it also got me thinking about everybody else.

I started paying more attention to how people act, how they think, and the little things they do. Sometimes I'd notice something in another person that reminded me of myself. When you recognize certain ADHD-related behaviors in your own life, you start noticing similar things in other people.

And then I'd wonder, are they like me? Do they think the way I do? Or am I looking at the world completely differently?

That's when I really started questioning the whole idea of being normal.

What are we even measuring that against? Who decided there's a certain way our brains are supposed to work? And if yours works differently, does that automatically mean something is wrong with it?

I don't think so.

I don't look at having ADHD symptoms as a curse. In some ways, I actually look at it as a blessing. There are things about the way my mind works that I appreciate. And just knowing more about it has helped me understand myself.

I'm still the same person I was before. I just have a better explanation for some of the things I've experienced.

That doesn't mean I suddenly have everything figured out.

But I don't feel like there's anything to be ashamed of, either.

I feel special. Not because I think I'm better than anybody else, but because I'm starting to understand that being different doesn't necessarily mean being wrong.

And I know there are plenty of other people out there who feel the same way.

That's where "Normal" came from. Just me thinking about how differently people experience the world and wondering why we're all so concerned with fitting into the same definition.

Am I normal?

I don't know.

I'm not even sure I know what that means anymore.
