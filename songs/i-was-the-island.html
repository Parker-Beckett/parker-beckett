Story Behind the Song

The Story Behind “I Was the Island”

The Things I Didn't Want to Do

People ask me sometimes how I did it.

How did I go from the life I was living to the life I have today?

And when I think about how to answer that, I think about the people I lost along the way.

The friends I pushed away. The relationships I damaged. How I'd gotten to a point where I'd pretty much isolated myself from everybody.

I was the island.

Looking back, I think about all the things I did that got me there. But I also think about what it took to turn things around.

And a lot of those things weren't necessarily what I wanted to do.

I had to start listening to other people. Actually listening. I went to sober living. I started doing things that, at the time, I probably would've rather avoided.

But they were things I needed to do.

That's an important part of the story for me. Because when somebody asks how I changed my life, I can't just tell them about where I ended up. I have to think about the things I did along the way, including the ones I wasn't particularly excited about doing.

I didn't always want to hear what other people had to say. And I certainly didn't always want to follow through on what they were suggesting.

But I started doing some of those things anyway.

And my life changed.

That's really where "I Was the Island" came from. Thinking about how I'd pushed so many people away, and how listening to other people eventually became part of what helped me turn my life around.

I don't have some complicated explanation for how I got here.

I can tell you what I did. I listened. I went to sober living. I did things I didn't necessarily want to do but needed to do.

And those things helped give me the life I have today.
